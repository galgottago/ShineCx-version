About

Bobbi Jo Smith

Senior BAS & DDC Controls Specialist with more than twenty years building, commissioning, and validating mission-critical systems. Work spans defense and naval facilities, aerospace, healthcare, data centers, government sites, and metrology laboratories.

Engagements include Honeywell, Precision Environments, Trane, MARTA, and Johnson Controls — platforms covering Honeywell, Trane, Niagara 4, and JCI Metasys, with integration across BACnet, Modbus, LonWorks, and native DDC.

ShineCX is her independent practice, based in Morgan Hill, California, and available for project-based commissioning, laboratory buildout, point-to-point validation, and controls programming.
